Mississippi State opened 2026 with a 62-13 win over UL Monroe in Starkville. The score is accurate. It also hides how early the game ended.

## When it stopped counting

X. Gayten's 14-yard touchdown run with 39 seconds left in the second quarter made it **41-3**. That 38-point margin crossed the standard [garbage-time line](/blog/the-plays-that-dont-count), and the game never came back under it.

That leaves only the first half to evaluate. **67 of the game's 146 scrimmage plays** came after that point, including all seven of J.J. Hill's carries for 118 yards and two touchdowns, and Marquis Johnson's 70-yard touchdown catch in the third quarter. All of it was real. None of it tells you much about either team.

UL Monroe's offense peaked late in the first quarter. After that it was a steady slide.

## Before garbage time

|                       | Plays | Success rate | EPA per play | Plays of 20+ yards |
| --------------------- | ----- | ------------ | ------------ | ------------------ |
| **Mississippi State** | 50    | **60%**      | **+0.68**    | 5                  |
| UL Monroe             | 29    | 28%          | −0.06        | 0                  |

Success rate is the cleanest number here. A play succeeds if it gains half the distance on first down, 70% on second, or all of it on third or fourth. Mississippi State hit that on six of every ten snaps and UL Monroe on fewer than three. UL Monroe did not have a single play of 20 yards before the break.

Read the EPA figures loosely. Fifty plays is a small sample, and 29 is smaller.

## Kamario Taylor

**22 of 34, 354 yards, 4 touchdowns, no interceptions, no sacks.**

Most of that came before garbage time: **21 of 32 for 284 yards and three touchdowns** in the first half. He succeeded on **62%** of those dropbacks and averaged **+0.58 EPA** per dropback. He added six first-half carries for 59 yards, including a 26-yard touchdown run on third down in the first quarter. His fourth touchdown pass was the 70-yarder to Johnson.

Parker Puckett finished 5 of 8 for 63 yards, all in garbage time.

For UL Monroe, Aidan Armenta was 9 of 14 for 61 yards in the first half, with a 27% success rate.
