Mississippi State went to Minneapolis and won 38-13. Unlike week 1, this one was a football game for most of the afternoon. Garbage time didn't start until the fourth quarter, and only **20 of 131 scrimmage plays** came after that point.

## How it went

Mississippi State scored on its opening drive: a 37-yard catch by Anthony Evans III on the second snap, then a 22-yard touchdown pass from Kamario Taylor to Z. Ragins. By halftime it was **28-6**, with touchdowns from Fluff Bothwell (5-yard run), S. Magee (30-yard catch) and Evans (15-yard catch).

Minnesota's 13 points break down like this: two field goals, and a touchdown its defense scored when J. Gerlach strip-sacked Taylor early in the third quarter and A. West recovered in the end zone. **Minnesota's offense scored six points.**

## The stand at the 4

Down 31-13, Minnesota drove into Mississippi State territory, helped by a 15-yard roughness penalty. Noah Jennings appeared to catch a touchdown at 4:35, but replay overturned it. Minnesota still got to 1st-and-goal at the 5.

Then J. Bledsoe sacked Drake Lindsey for an 8-yard loss. Two plays later, facing 4th-and-4, Lindsey found J. Tracy short, and K. Singleton tackled him at the 4. Turnover on downs, with 2:33 left in the third quarter.

Bothwell's 3-yard touchdown early in the fourth made it 38-13.

## Before garbage time

Minnesota ran more plays and gained almost nothing for them. It dropped back on 47 of its 58 snaps before garbage time, and its leading rusher, Darius Taylor, finished with 6 carries for 15 yards. A 41% success rate with only three plays of 20 yards is an offense moving the chains occasionally and never hurting anyone.

## Kamario Taylor

**16 of 22, 227 yards, 3 touchdowns, no interceptions, 1 sack.**

Before garbage time he was 14 of 19 for 211 yards and all three touchdowns, averaging **+0.55 EPA** per dropback. That's lower volume than week 1 but just as efficient. The one real blemish was the strip-sack.

Lindsey finished **31 of 48 for 279 yards**, with no touchdowns and two sacks. Before garbage time that worked out to +0.09 EPA per dropback, which is a lot of completions for very little.

## Who did the damage

Mississippi State's top contributors before garbage time, by total EPA:

Bothwell finished with **18 carries for 113 yards and two touchdowns**, and every carry came before garbage time. Evans had 7 catches for 121 yards and a touchdown.

Before garbage time, Mississippi State ran on 33 of its 53 snaps and dropped back on 20, close to the mirror image of Minnesota's 11 runs and 47 dropbacks.

**Biggest play:** Taylor to Evans for 37 yards on the game's second Mississippi State snap, worth **+3.4** expected points. It set up the opening touchdown.
