Hi, I'm Minsub. I'm a Mississippi State graduate, and I'm still at MSU studying data
science.

This blog is where those two interests meet. College football is the dataset I use to
practice the work I want to get good at: analysis, visualization, building pipelines, and
modeling. Every post starts as a question I can't answer from the box score, and ends when
I've either found something or convinced myself I haven't.

Football knowledge is the other half of it. Reading the game well and reading the numbers
well are different skills, and I'm trying to build both at once. Writing things down forces
me to defend a claim with evidence instead of a hunch, which is most of the point.

If you spot an error or want to argue with a conclusion, email me. I'd rather be corrected
than confidently wrong.
